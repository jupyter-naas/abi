# S4 — logistics

**Reserved. No modules yet.**

## The question this bucket answers

> *What physical or contracted resources do we need, and where are they?*

In the staff system, S4 is explicitly **physical**: materiel, transport, facilities, services and
medical support. In a civilian organization it covers procurement, supply chain, asset
management, facilities and vendor management.

## What belongs here

- Procurement and purchasing
- Supplier and vendor management
- Inventory and asset tracking
- Facilities management
- Shipping and fulfilment
- Expense and travel management

## What does not belong here

Anything whose subject is **information rather than goods**. Document ingestion, data pipelines
and knowledge management are [`signals/`](../signals/) (S6) — they move bytes, not boxes. This is
why the `document` module is filed under signals.

See [`../AGENT.md`](../AGENT.md) for the full filing rules and [`../README.md`](../README.md) for
the framework.
