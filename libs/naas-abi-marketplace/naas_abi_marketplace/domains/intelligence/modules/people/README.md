# people — S2 intelligence

> *What can be known about a person, whoever employs them?*

People intelligence: the vocabulary, pipelines and apps for what is published
about any person - where they worked and studied, the roles and missions they
held, what they know, hold, speak and follow, and how they are presented. It
applies to a candidate, a client's CFO or a competitor's partner exactly as it
applies to your own staff.

A loadable module of the `intelligence` bucket, next to
[`organizations/`](../organizations/) and [`wsr/`](../wsr/). See
[`../../../README.md`](../../../README.md) for the staff system and
[`../../../AGENT.md`](../../../AGENT.md) for the filing rules.

## Generic here, specific in personnel

This module holds what any source can say about anyone. What an organization
records about **its own staff** - the employee role, the job position it
defined, the employment record and contract, remuneration, grade, service line -
is the personnel (S1) module's, and it **specializes** these classes rather
than redefining them:

| people (generic) | personnel (internal) |
|---|---|
| `abi:ActOfWorking` ⊑ CCO Planned Act | `abi:ActOfEmployment` ⊑ `abi:ActOfWorking`, CCO Act of Employment |
| `abi:OccupationRole` ≡ CCO Occupation Role | `abi:EmployeeRole` ⊑ `abi:OccupationRole` |
| `abi:employment_type` - the engagement type a source publishes | `abi:EmploymentContract`, `abi:contract_type` |
| `abi:worksFor` - a source says they work there | `abi:isEmployedBy` - the employer's own record |

The dependency runs one way: `PersonnelOntology` imports `PeopleOntology`, the
personnel pipelines extend `PeopleGraphContext`, the cockpit embeds
`graph_page/`. Nothing here imports, queries or names a personnel IRI.

**Same individual, two graphs.** An act of working is minted once, in
`people:`, and written to `…/graph/people`. When it is also an act of
employment, personnel types the same IRI `abi:ActOfEmployment` and adds
its contract in `…/graph/personnel`. `act_of_working_uri` in
`pipelines/utils/graph_builders.py` is the shared key.

## Vocabulary

`PeopleOntology.ttl` holds the shared classes; each process slice defines one
BFO process and restricts them.

| Class | Bucket | Carries |
|---|---|---|
| `abi:Person` | WHO | The human being. Survives any change of employer. |
| `abi:OccupationRole` | WHY | The role a person bears while working in some capacity; its `job_title` as the source states it. |
| `abi:StudentRole` | WHY | The role borne while enrolled in a curriculum. |
| `abi:Mission` | HOW WE KNOW | What a person is charged with in one engagement; concretized by the occupation role. |
| `abi:ProfileSummary` | HOW WE KNOW | Headline, summary, quote and the years of experience a source claims. |
| `abi:Portrait` | HOW WE KNOW | The address of a photograph (CCO Image subclass). Never the bytes. |
| `abi:ProfileDocument` | HOW WE KNOW | The page a claim was published on. Every slice points its records back to one. |
| `abi:EnrollmentRecord`, `abi:AcademicDegree` | HOW WE KNOW | A course of study and the degree it awarded. |
| `abi:Certification` | HOW WE KNOW | A certification or licence (CCO Certificate), its issuer, dates and credential URL. |
| `abi:Recommendation` | HOW WE KNOW | What one person wrote about another. Both people are required. |
| `abi:Skill` | HOW IT IS | A learned capacity a person bears; any planned act can develop one (`developsSkill`). |
| `abi:LanguageCapability` | HOW IT IS | A language and the proficiency a source stated. |
| `abi:Interest` | HOW IT IS | What someone follows outside the duties of any one job. |

`abi:Site` additionally carries `office_label`, `city_name`, `country_name` and
`country_code`. Each act occurs in an `abi:GeospatialRegion` (a site: the
street-level `abi:GeospatialLocation`, a city or a country, chained upwards by
`abi:continuantPartOf`). The CCO facility (`OfficeBuilding`,
`EducationalFacility`, `Facility`) is a material entity located in that site
(`abi:locatedIn some abi:GeospatialLocation`), tied to the person and
organization by restriction in the slice.

## Process ledger

The `processes/` folder tracks entries of the
[NCOR BFO-Process-Ledger](https://github.com/NCOR-Organization/BFO-Process-Ledger)
(`bpl-processes/personnel`; the upstream category is broader than S1 - the
entries below are about any person, so they live here).

| Entry | What it records |
|---|---|
| `ActOfWorkingProcess.ttl` | Performing work for an organization |
| `ActOfStudyingProcess.ttl` | Acquiring a curriculum at an educational organization |
| `ActOfCertificationProcess.ttl` | A certifying organization attesting a competence or licence |
| `ActOfProfilingProcess.ttl` | Registering a person from a profile document (orchestrates the three above and the profile pipeline) |

The HR entries (onboarding, separation, identity verification, residence
change) belong to personnel.

## Pipelines

All build on `PeopleGraphContext` and persist only the triples they added:

| Pipeline | Tool | Writes |
|---|---|---|
| `ActOfWorkingPipeline` | `register_act_of_working` | One job: the act, organization, client, site, temporal region, occupation role, mission, employment type, skills |
| `ActOfStudyingPipeline` | `register_act_of_studying` | One course of study: the act, educational organization, enrollment record, degree |
| `ActOfCertificationPipeline` | `register_act_of_certification` | One certification and the act that awarded it |
| `PersonProfilePipeline` | `register_person_profile` | Person-level facts: summary, portrait, contact details, work location, the organization they work for (`worksFor`), certifications, languages, interests, recommendations |
| `ProfileFromSourcePipeline` | `register_profile_from_source` | One published source payload, all of the above in order |

`ActOfWorkingPipeline` accepts `contract_type` as an alias of
`employment_type`, so sources written before the split still load.

## People Search (`apps/people`)

```
data/demo/person/*/index.json  ->  pipelines  ->  graphs/demo/people.ttl
  ->  competency queries  ->  dataset service (9 typed tables)  ->  apps/people
```

```bash
make people        # rebuild the graph and the datasets, then serve
make app-people    # serve what is already exported
```

The app reads the `people` dataset namespace and mounts at `/api/people`
(Nexus app id `naas_abi_marketplace.domains.intelligence.modules.people:people`).
A profile opens as a resume or as the **person graph** (`apps/people/graph_page/`):
the 7-bucket canvas, occurrents and continuants apart, one zone per BFO bucket.
See `apps/people/README.md` and `apps/people/AGENTS.md`.

## Queries

`PeopleSparqlQueries.ttl`, loaded by name in `PeopleAgent.get_sparql_tools()`:

| Query | Answers | Arguments |
|---|---|---|
| `find_working_experiences` | Acts of working (person, org, client, site, period, role, job title, mission, employment type, source) | `limit` |
| `find_skills_developed` | Skills with the act of working each was developed in | `limit` |
| `find_educations` | Acts of studying | `limit` |
| `find_people_directory` | One row per person for a directory | `limit` |
| `find_profile_header` | One person's profile header, by slug | `person_slug` |
| `find_person_skills` | Which skills each person bears | `limit` |
| `find_certifications` | Certifications and licences people hold | `limit` |
| `find_acts_of_certification` | How each certification was awarded | `limit` |
| `find_languages` | Languages and stated proficiency | `limit` |
| `find_recommendations` | Recommendations and their authors | `limit` |
| `find_interests` | What people follow | `limit` |

Adding a query to the TTL is not enough: register its label in
`PeopleAgent.get_sparql_tools()`.

## Regenerating

After editing a TTL here:

```bash
for f in ActOfWorking ActOfStudying ActOfCertification ActOfProfiling; do
  uv run python -m naas_abi_core.utils.onto2py \
    libs/naas-abi-marketplace/naas_abi_marketplace/domains/intelligence/modules/people/ontologies/processes/${f}Process.ttl
done
uv run python -m naas_abi_core.utils.onto2py \
  libs/naas-abi-marketplace/naas_abi_marketplace/domains/intelligence/modules/people/ontologies/modules/PeopleOntology.ttl
```

Then regenerate the personnel ontology too: it imports this one.
