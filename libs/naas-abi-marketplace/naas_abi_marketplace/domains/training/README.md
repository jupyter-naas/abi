# S7 — training

**Reserved. No modules yet.**

## The question this bucket answers

> *How do our people become capable of their work?*

In the staff system, S7 organizes and supervises training of the force. In a civilian
organization it covers onboarding, enablement, certification and internal education.

## What belongs here

- Employee onboarding
- Skills assessment and enablement
- Certification and compliance training tracking
- Curriculum and course generation
- Internal knowledge testing

## What does not belong here

Producing content for an **external** audience is [`external/`](../external/) (S9). Training is
inward-facing — it builds the organization's own capability.

Managing *who* people are and their employment status is [`personnel/`](../personnel/) (S1);
training is about what they can *do*.

See [`../AGENT.md`](../AGENT.md) for the full filing rules and [`../README.md`](../README.md) for
the framework.
