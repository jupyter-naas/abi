# S1 - personnel

> *Who is in the organization, and what is their status?*

What an organization records about **its own staff**: the employee role a
person bears while employed, the job position it fills, the employment record
and contract that document the relationship, and the status, remuneration,
grade and service line the organization assigns.

What can be known about any person - their career, studies, skills,
certifications, published profile - is people intelligence:
[`intelligence/modules/people`](../intelligence/modules/people/). Personnel
**specializes** that vocabulary and never redefines it. See
[`../README.md`](../README.md) for the staff system and
[`../AGENT.md`](../AGENT.md) for the filing rules.

## Vocabulary

`PersonnelOntology.ttl` keeps three things apart that HR systems habitually merge:

| Class | Bucket | Why it is separate |
|---|---|---|
| `abi:Person` | WHO | The human being. Survives any change of employer. |
| `personnel:EmployeeRole` ⊑ `people:OccupationRole` | WHY | Inheres in the person *while employed*. Ends without the person ending. |
| `personnel:EmploymentRecord` | HOW WE KNOW | The document reporting the relationship. Copyable between systems. |

`personnel:JobPosition` is deliberately **not** a BFO role: an open requisition
has no occupant, and a position that vanished whenever it fell vacant could not
be advertised, budgeted or reported on. It is a generically dependent continuant
concretized by `personnel:EmployeeRole` via `personnel:hasJobPosition`.

| Class | Bucket | Carries |
|---|---|---|
| `personnel:ActOfEmployment` | WHAT | An act of working for the employing organization: realizes an employee role, concretizes a contract. |
| `personnel:JobDescription` | HOW WE KNOW | The published description of a position. |
| `personnel:EmploymentContract` | HOW WE KNOW | The terms the person works under (`contract_type`). |
| `personnel:EmploymentStatus` | HOW IT IS | `active` / `on-leave` / `notice-period` / `terminated`. |
| `personnel:Remuneration` | HOW IT IS | What the person is paid. |
| `personnel:Grade` | HOW IT IS | The seniority the organization recognises, apart from any position title. |
| `personnel:ServiceLine` | WHO | An organization that is a member part of the employer. |

## Same individual, two graphs

An act of employment **is** the act of working the people graph already holds.
`PersonnelGraphContext.add_employment` mints nothing new for the act: it types
the people IRI (`act_of_working_uri`) `personnel:ActOfEmployment`, types its
occupation role `personnel:EmployeeRole`, and adds the position, contract and
remuneration. Those triples go to `http://ontology.naas.ai/graph/personnel`;
the act, its mission and skills stay in `…/graph/people`.

The personnel graph also holds each person's identity (type and label) next to
their records, so the HR queries read one graph.

## Process ledger roadmap

Internal entries of the
[NCOR BFO-Process-Ledger](https://github.com/NCOR-Organization/BFO-Process-Ledger)
personnel category. Working, studying, certification and profiling are about
any person and live in the people module.

| Status | Entry | What it records |
|:---:|---|---|
| ✅ | `ActOfEmploymentProcess.ttl` | Working for the employing organization, as it records it |
| ☐ | `IdentityVerificationProcess.ttl` | Establishing and validating a person's identity |
| ☐ | `ResidenceChangeProcess.ttl` | Recording a change of location or address |
| ☐ | `OnboardingProcess.ttl` | Integration of personnel into an organization |
| ☐ | `SeparationProcess.ttl` | Departure of personnel from an organization |

## Pipelines

| Pipeline | Tool | Writes |
|---|---|---|
| `ActOfEmploymentPipeline` | `register_act_of_employment` | Employee role, job position, contract and remuneration on an act of working (register the act itself with `register_act_of_working`) |
| `PersonnelProfilePipeline` | `register_personnel_profile` | That the organization employs the person, their service line and grade |

Run `PersonnelProfilePipeline` after the acts of employment for the same
person: a service line attaches to the employee roles those create. The person
is also recorded as a member part of the service line, so the membership
survives someone whose employment has not been recorded.

## Demo graph + cockpit datasets

The people module's demo files hold what each fictional person publishes.
This module's own files, `data/demo/person/<slug>/index.json` (same slugs), hold
what their employer records about them, and only this module reads them:

| Key | Becomes |
|---|---|
| `employer`, `service_line`, `grade` | `isEmployedBy`, the service line (with its employee roles), `hasGrade` |
| `employments[]` | the people act of working named by `organization` / `client` / `title`, typed `ActOfEmployment`, with job position, contract and remuneration |
| `roster` | employment record, job description, employment status |

```bash
make demo-graph   # people demo + employer records -> graphs/demo/personnel.ttl
make demo-data    # graph -> SPARQL export -> apps/cockpit/data + ObjectStorage
```

`mode=triple_store` inserts only the employer records into the personnel graph;
build the people graph with the people module's `build_demo_person_graph`.

The cockpit's graph page is the people module's `apps/people/graph_page/`,
served at `/api/personnel-cockpit/graph-page/` (`__init__.py` and the cockpit
dev server mount it). The cockpit feeds it HR roster rows through
`apps/cockpit/graph_query.graph_page_roster`.

## Queries

`PersonnelSparqlQueries.ttl`, loaded by name in `PersonnelAgent.get_sparql_tools()`:

| Query | Answers | Arguments |
|---|---|---|
| `find_active_employees` | Who works here right now? | `limit` |
| `find_employee_by_id` | Everything on one person, by employee id | `employee_id` |
| `find_employees_by_status` | Who is on leave / serving notice? | `status_value`, `limit` |
| `find_employees_by_organization` | Who works at which legal entity? | `organization_name`, `limit` |
| `find_employee_roster` | Full roster with every status | `limit` |
| `find_open_job_positions` | What are we hiring for? | `limit` |
| `find_positions_by_title` | Who fills the positions matching a title? | `job_title`, `limit` |
| `find_headcount_by_job_family` | How is the org split across disciplines? | `limit` |

Headcount is computed from employment records with no `termination_date`.
Counting `abi:Person` gives everyone ever recorded; counting `EmploymentRecord`
double-counts rehires and inter-entity transfers.
