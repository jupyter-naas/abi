# S3 · Operations

> *How do we execute the mission we are on right now?*

This folder holds the **current, committed work**: everything needed to deliver today. It is
usually the largest folder, because most of what an organization does at any moment is executing
work already decided.

## What to store here

- Engagement and project files: scopes, deliverables, status reports, meeting notes
- Sales execution: proposals, pipelines, account plans, call notes
- Client delivery and support: tickets, incident reports, service reviews
- Running campaigns and their performance tracking
- Procedures and checklists used to carry out the work

## What goes elsewhere

- Work that is not committed yet (strategy, roadmaps, next campaigns): `plans/`.
- Analyses of the market or of a client before the work starts: `intelligence/`.
- Contracts and invoices as financial records: `finance/`.

When a project ends, keep its closing report here and move lessons for the future to `plans/`.
