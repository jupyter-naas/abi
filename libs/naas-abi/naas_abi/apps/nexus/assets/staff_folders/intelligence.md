# S2 · Intelligence

> *What is true about the world outside us?*

This folder holds what the organization **collects and analyses about its environment**: markets,
competitors, clients, people and events. Intelligence observes. It does not decide what to do (that
is `plans/`) and it does not act on the finding (that is `operations/`).

## What to store here

- Market studies, sector and competitor analyses, benchmarks
- Client and prospect research, due-diligence and background files
- Profiles of organizations and people as seen from public sources
- Open-source collections: articles, reports, filings, datasets and their sources
- Risk assessments, watch lists and situation reports

## What goes elsewhere

- The organization's own staff records: `personnel/`.
- Decisions and strategies drawn from the analysis: `plans/`.
- Material produced to be published outside: `external/`.

Keep the source with each finding (link, date, document): an analysis is only as good as what it
can trace back to.
