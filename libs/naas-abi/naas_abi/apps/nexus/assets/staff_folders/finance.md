# S8 · Finance

> *Where does the money go, and what is it worth?*

This folder holds **recorded financial facts**: transactions, balances, budgets and contractual
obligations. If the subject of a file is a figure in the ledger, it belongs here.

## What to store here

- Accounting records: journals, ledgers, closing packs, statutory accounts
- Treasury: bank statements, cash forecasts, financing
- Budgets, forecasts, controlling and management reporting
- Invoices, purchase and sales contracts as financial obligations
- Audit files, tax returns and financial policies

## What goes elsewhere

- The organization's people and their contracts of employment: `personnel/`.
- Strategy and the plans a budget serves: `plans/`.
- Financial analyses of **other** organizations (competitors, targets): `intelligence/`.

Financial files are often confidential. Check who can read this workspace before adding them.
