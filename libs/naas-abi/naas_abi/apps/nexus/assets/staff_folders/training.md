# S7 · Training

> *How do our people become capable of their work?*

This folder holds what helps people **learn and stay qualified**: onboarding, enablement,
certification and internal education.

## What to store here

- Onboarding paths and welcome kits
- Courses, workshops, slide decks and exercises
- Skills frameworks, assessments and knowledge tests
- Certification and compliance-training tracking
- Recordings and notes from internal sessions

## What goes elsewhere

- Content produced for an **external** audience: `external/`.
- The organization's staff records (who holds which role or grade): `personnel/`.
- Reference documentation that is not teaching material: `signals/`.

This function is often empty at first. An empty folder simply shows a part of the organization the
workspace does not cover yet.
