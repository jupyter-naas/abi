# S4 · Logistics

> *What physical or contracted resources do we need, and where are they?*

This folder holds the **material side** of the organization: what is bought, supplied, stored,
shipped or maintained, and the vendors that provide it.

## What to store here

- Procurement and purchasing files: requests, quotes, purchase orders
- Supplier and vendor records, framework agreements, vendor reviews
- Inventory and asset registers, equipment and licence tracking
- Facilities: offices, sites, leases, maintenance
- Shipping and fulfilment, expense and travel arrangements

## What goes elsewhere

- Documents and knowledge are information, not goods: `signals/`.
- The cost of what is bought, as ledger entries and budgets: `finance/`.
- Software engineering and IT systems themselves: `signals/`.

This function is often empty at first. An empty folder simply shows a part of the organization the
workspace does not cover yet.
