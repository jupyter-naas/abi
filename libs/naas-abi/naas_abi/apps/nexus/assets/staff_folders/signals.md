# S6 · Signals

> *How does information move and get stored inside the organization?*

This folder holds the **plumbing of information**: the systems, data and knowledge bases the
organization uses to talk to itself and keep what it knows. Signals is about the pipes, not the
message; information about the outside world belongs in `intelligence/`.

## What to store here

- Knowledge bases, document libraries, templates and reference material
- Data exports, data dictionaries and data-quality notes
- IT and tooling documentation: architectures, access procedures, runbooks
- Internal communication material: newsletters, announcements, channel guides
- Ontologies, vocabularies and naming conventions shared across the workspace

## What goes elsewhere

- Findings about markets, competitors or people outside: `intelligence/`.
- Equipment, licences and vendors as purchased resources: `logistics/`.
- Training content built from this knowledge: `training/`.

Documents placed here are good candidates for search and for the assistants: keep them clean,
dated and in readable formats.
