# S9 · External

> *How do we relate to people and bodies outside the organization?*

This folder holds the organization's **relations with a broad outside audience**: community, brand,
public affairs, partnerships and external communications. It is defined by the counterparty: an
audience rather than a paying client (clients are served in `operations/`).

## What to store here

- Published content: articles, posts, press releases, brochures, videos
- Brand guidelines, logos and visual assets
- Event, sponsorship and partnership files
- Public-affairs and institutional relations material
- Community management: editorial calendars, audience feedback, social media reports

## What goes elsewhere

- Work for a paying client (proposals, deliveries, support): `operations/`.
- The strategy behind upcoming campaigns: `plans/`.
- Studies of the outside world that are not meant to be published: `intelligence/`.

Anything here may end up public: keep a clear final version and its approval with it.
