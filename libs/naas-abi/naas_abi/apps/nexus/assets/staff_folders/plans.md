# S5 · Plans

> *What should we do next, and how will we get there?*

This folder holds **future work**: the decisions about what the organization will do once the
current work ends. Its defining property is time. As soon as work is committed and running, it moves
to `operations/`.

## What to store here

- Strategy documents, ambitions and objectives
- Roadmaps, planning cycles and annual or quarterly plans
- Business cases, scenarios and options under study
- Designs of upcoming campaigns, offers or programmes
- Lessons learned that should shape the next plan

## What goes elsewhere

- Work that is committed and running: `operations/`.
- The analyses a plan relies on (markets, competitors, clients): `intelligence/`.
- Budgets once approved, as financial records: `finance/`.

A good plan states what it decides, for when, and what would make it change.
