# S1 · Personnel

> *Who is in the organization, and what is their status?*

This folder holds what the organization records about **its own people**: who works here, in which
role and position, under which contract, grade and service line, and how that changes over time.

## What to store here

- Staff lists, organization charts and position descriptions
- Employment records: contracts, amendments, status changes, departures
- Staffing plans, recruitment files and onboarding paperwork
- Remuneration, grades, reviews and awards
- HR policies and people-ops procedures

## What goes elsewhere

- What can be known about **anyone**, inside or outside (career, studies, published profile, skills
  seen from the outside): `intelligence/`.
- Courses, certifications and enablement material: `training/`.
- Payroll as a ledger (journals, payments, budgets): `finance/`.

Personnel files are often sensitive. Keep personal data to what the work needs, and check who can
read this workspace before adding it.
